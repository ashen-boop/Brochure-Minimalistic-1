◆ About
◈ Foundation

Founded in 2014 by Ava Chen and Maya Patel — formerly of Pentagram (London) and Collins (New York) — The Gallery is a design studio built on the belief that visual storytelling should be both rigorous and generous.

◈ Approach

Collaborative and iterative — we uncover the heart of your brand and translate it into design that resonates across every touchpoint, from print to pixel.

◈ Philosophy

We're strategists, storytellers, and brand advocates. Great design is invisible — intuitive, effortless, and built to last beyond the trend cycle.

◇ Vision
◈ Meaningful Design

We envision design as a meaningful dialogue between brand and audience. Our mission is to elevate human connection through intentional, considered work.

◈ Storytelling

We believe the best design tells a story — one that resonates emotionally, intellectually, and aesthetically, across every medium.

◈ Design for Good

We're committed to inclusive, accessible, and sustainable design that inspires change — and we donate 5% of annual profits to arts education.

◈ Our Story

What began as a two-person studio in a shared Hackney workspace has grown into a 14-person practice with offices in London and Berlin. Shaped by curiosity, collaboration, and an obsession with craft.

Over a decade, we've worked with 280+ clients — from early-stage startups to heritage brands like Lumina and Artisan. Each project has reinforced our belief in design's quiet power.

✦ What We Stand For

We believe design should be intentional, inclusive, and impactful. Our values guide every decision we make — from the first sketch to the final delivery.

  • Design with purpose — Every element serves a strategic goal.
  • People first — We design for real humans with real needs.
  • Craft over speed — We'd rather do it right than do it fast.

◇ The Creative Process

Our process is transparent, collaborative, and adaptable. The best work emerges from a deep understanding of your brand, audience, and commercial goals.

  • 01. Discover — Research, listen, and understand.
  • 02. Define — Strategic direction and creative brief.
  • 03. Design — Iterate, refine, and present.
  • 04. Deliver — Rollout, guidelines, and support.

◈ FAQs

◇ What services do you offer?

Brand identity, print & digital, motion graphics, UI/UX, and creative direction — all tailored to your needs.

◇ How long does a project take?

Timelines vary. Brand identity: 6–10 weeks. Smaller projects: 2–4 weeks. We'll give you a detailed schedule after our first call.

◇ Do you work with startups?

Absolutely. We work with companies of all sizes — from early-stage startups to established enterprises like Lumina and Artisan.

◇ What is your design process?

Discovery, Definition, Design, and Delivery. We collaborate closely at every stage and keep you informed throughout.
